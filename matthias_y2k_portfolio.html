** ABOUT ME **

Hello and welcome to my homepage!! I am a geospatial data scientist and urban researcher with a background spanning land surveying, photogrammetry, remote sensing, and computational fluid dynamics. My PhD at NYU Tandon focused on microclimate modeling and GeoAI applications in dense urban environments!!

My work sits at the intersection of physical simulation and machine learning — using CFD to generate ground-truth thermal fields, then training spatial models to predict and generalize at city scale. I have worked across Germany, Singapore, Abu Dhabi, and New York City!!

I am looking 4 roles combining geospatial engineering, climate risk analysis, and applied AI :)
